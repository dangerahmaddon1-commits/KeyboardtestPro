How to Test Your Keyboard Online

Our free online keyboard test utility allows you to instantly check every key on your laptop or desktop computer keyboard without downloading any software. Simply press any key to test hardware responsiveness, sticky switches, and key mapping.

Key Ghosting & N-Key Rollover (NKRO) Test

Keyboard ghosting happens when certain key combinations fail to register when multiple keys are pressed at the exact same time. Use our active rollover counter to test your gaming keyboard for multi-key anti-ghosting capabilities.

Compatible Laptop & Mechanical Keyboard Layouts

  • Standard Laptop Keyboards: Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, and Apple MacBooks.
  • Desktop & Mechanical Switches: Full-Size (108 keys) and Tenkeyless (TKL) layouts with Cherry MX, Gateron, or custom mechanical switches.
